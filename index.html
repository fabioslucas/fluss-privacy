FLUSS Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026 · App: FLUSS (com.fluss.app)

In short: FLUSS works without an account. Your activities, routes, sensor data, emergency contacts and safety incidents are stored only on your phone. FLUSS has no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own, and it does not sell or share your data.

Who we are

FLUSS is an independent app for recording movement activities (running, walking, cycling and more), viewing live sensor data, and asking for help with FLUSS Safety. Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact email shown on the FLUSS page in Google Play.

Data FLUSS uses and why

  • Precise location (GPS) — to record your route, distance, pace and speed while you record an activity (including with the screen locked, shown by a persistent notification), while the Sensors screen is open, and during a FLUSS Safety alert to show your last known position. Location is not collected at any other time.
  • Motion and environment sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, barometer) — shown live on the Sensors screen. They are saved with an activity only if you turn on sensor recording; step counts are estimated from the accelerometer.
  • Emergency contacts — names and phone numbers you choose from your contacts or type in. FLUSS only receives the contact you pick; it does not read your address book.
  • Microphone (optional) — only if you turn on the emergency audio check. During an alert where you ask for help or do not respond, FLUSS records up to 30 seconds of audio, with a visible indicator. The recording stays on your phone and is deleted automatically after the period you choose (1, 7 or 30 days).
  • Battery level — read during an alert to save battery and noted in the incident log.
  • Notifications — to show an active alert and notices after the app restarts.

Where your data is stored

Everything above is stored in the app's private storage on your device. FLUSS does not upload your routes, sensor data, contacts, safety incidents or recordings, and it has no account system or backend.

When data leaves your phone

  • Map tiles — to draw maps, the app downloads map images of the area on screen from the map provider (OpenStreetMap, or Mapbox if configured). The provider can see your IP address and the approximate area being viewed. Your recorded route is never sent.
  • Only when you choose: sharing an activity image, sharing an audio check, calling 192 or a contact, or sending a message to a contact. FLUSS opens your dialer, messaging or share app with the content pre-filled; you decide whether to send it, and that app's own privacy policy applies. Emergency messages can include a map link with your last known location if that option is on.
  • Contribution link — if you tap "Make a contribution", the page opens in your browser.

What FLUSS does not do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal data.
  • No background location when you are not recording, viewing Sensors or in an alert.
  • No automatic calls or messages.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete single activities or incidents, delete all activity data in Settings › Privacy and data, delete emergency contacts, or uninstall the app, which removes everything stored by FLUSS. Audio checks are also deleted automatically after their retention period.

Permissions

You can allow or revoke location, notifications and microphone at any time in your phone's settings. FLUSS keeps working without them, with the related features unavailable.

Safety notice

FLUSS Safety helps you ask for help but cannot guarantee that an accident is detected, that a call or message goes through, or that help arrives. In an emergency, call 192 (SAMU) or your local emergency number directly.

Children

FLUSS is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
